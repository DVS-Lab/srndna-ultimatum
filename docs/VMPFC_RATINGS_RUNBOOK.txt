Corrected vmPFC ROI and three-partner ratings figures
===================================================

This workflow is independent of the running DMN/PALM audit. It never writes
PALM scratch, reruns FEAT, changes L1/L2 files, or replaces historical figures.
Run on Linux1 with the srndna-ultimatum conda environment:

  bash code/run_vmpfc_ratings_batch.sh

Commit results/vmpfc_ratings/v1, including attempt logs on failure.
The workload is serial and single-threaded. No --jobs flag is needed.

vmPFC inputs and checks
----------------------
Use the exact historical masks/roi-VMPFC.nii.gz, documented in masks/README.md
as the Feng et al. (2015) fair>unfair location [6,46,-12]. Do not redraw a new
sphere: the supplied mask contains 19 voxels on its original grid. The mask
is independent of the current statistical maps, not selected for significance.

Take corrected activation L2 COPE2/4/6 (computer/similar/dissimilar offer-size
slopes) for the locked 47 participants, from the three completed pipelines:
full display + all-response RT height, decision + pooled post-response, and
decision + partner-specific post-response. No timing is selected by outcome.
Paths come from retained revision_jobs.tsv manifests and must agree with the
retained activation group inputs. Prepared L1 FSF hashes, contrast labels,
original weights and compiled contrast vectors are checked for all 282 runs.
L2 prepared FSF hashes are checked. Input/output hashes are recorded.

Every ROI voxel must have positive coverage in each condition's L2 mask.
Zero COPE values remain valid data. No nonzero-only mean or silent trimming.
Nearest-neighbour mask resampling is allowed only to a common image grid;
different participant/model grids and incomplete coverage stop the run.

Statistics and figures
----------------------
For each timing report unadjusted and nuisance-adjusted analyses. Adjustment
uses sex, tSNR, mean FD, and corrected mean RT from the existing locked tables,
verified against retained activation group covariates. Center nuisance columns
over all 47 participants. Do not add behavioral slopes or norm parameters.

Keep three measurements per participant paired by using participant-level
outcomes: average offer slope; each partner's slope; human mean minus computer;
and similar minus dissimilar. Fit each outcome against centered age group,
with/without nuisance columns. The age coefficient is older minus younger.
Use unweighted OLS estimates with heteroscedasticity-robust HC3 SEs and a
t(n-p) reference for scalar tests (an approximation, not an exact small-sample
test). The joint age-by-partner test uses the two independent partner contrasts
and their subject-vector HC3 covariance, with an asymptotic chi-square(2)
reference. This avoids treating repeated measures as independent or imposing
sphericity. No result depends on completion of voxelwise permutation inference.

Report raw p values AND Holm adjustment across timings and adjustment variants
within declared families: age averaged across partners, age by condition,
age-interaction components, partner differences, and offer slopes versus zero.
All scalar tests are two-sided; inspect the coefficient sign for direction.
The six joint interaction tests form a separate Holm family. This is an
exploratory ROI reanalysis, not proof that the original slide's age claim holds.
Influence/leave-one-out estimates are reported without participant exclusions.

Each timing has a six-bar raw plot and a six-bar adjusted plot. Adjusted values
subtract fitted nuisance contributions separately by condition while retaining
age-group effects. Error bars are participant SD/sqrt(n), NOT 95% intervals,
HC3 model SEs or FLAME SEs. They describe dispersion of plotted values and do
not propagate uncertainty in estimating the adjustment. Read inference from
the tables, not from overlap of bars. No inverse-VARCOPE weighting is used:
the mean of voxelwise VARCOPEs is not the variance of an ROI average without
accounting for spatial covariance. COPE units differ across timing models;
do not interpret a larger number across timings as a larger biological effect.

Ratings
-------
Use existing audited last_complete_block summaries, age_group=all, N=42 at
each time point, with 41 paired participants. Primary figure: pre/post fairness
and likeability, each showing computer, similar and dissimilar partners.
Companion figure: post-task anger and satisfaction. Means +/- participant SEM,
fixed 0-10 axes, no invented pre-task affect measurements or repeated-session
counts. Retained paired tests/FDR values are copied, not recalculated from
summary means. Pre/post samples are not identical; these are levels, not
evidence that the task produced a change. Similarity is relative to own age.

Reproducibility
---------------
Completed outputs are checksum-verified and skipped. Changed inputs/code stop
instead of mixing versions. Large imaging files stay on Linux1; small tables,
PNG/SVG figures, receipts and logs are committed. The plotting code can run on
the local clone using only the already committed ratings summaries.

HC3 definition:
https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.regression.linear_model.OLSResults.HC3_se.html
Holm correction:
https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.stats.multitest.multipletests.html
