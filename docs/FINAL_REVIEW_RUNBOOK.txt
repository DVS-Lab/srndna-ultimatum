PHASE 1 — ESTIMABILITY, INFLUENCE, SIMPLE EFFECTS, AND DEWEIGHTING

Start with the dry run:

python3 code/run_revision_completion.py --phase quick

If that completes without an error, execute:

python3 code/run_revision_completion.py --phase quick --execute

This first checks all 282 original and six repaired L1 design bundles. It stops
on incomplete coverage, residual rank problems, or focal estimability flags.
Empty nuisance columns are reported separately. All saved contrasts are audited;
the focal checks concern copes 4, 6, and 7 at both numerical cutoffs.

It then runs two L3 models, with at most two concurrent FEAT processes:
  simple-effects: original four contrasts plus both negative simple effects;
  outlier-deweighted: same six contrasts, full sample, robust_yn=1.

Contrast order is fixed:
  1 younger similar-minus-dissimilar positive
  2 older similar-minus-dissimilar positive
  3 younger-minus-older interaction
  4 older-minus-younger interaction
  5 younger similar-minus-dissimilar negative
  6 older similar-minus-dissimilar negative

All inputs, group covariates, and FLAME 1+2 settings are retained. No participant
is removed. The inference remains Z>3.1, cluster p<.05 within each contrast;
these extra tests are not corrected as one family across every new contrast.
Because FLAME 1+2 includes stochastic estimation and added contrasts can alter
which voxels receive stage-2 evaluation, do not demand bitwise identity with the
four-contrast primary fit. Report the original primary result and clearly label
these new sensitivity fits. The paired new six-contrast models share the same
contrast specification, isolating the outlier-deweighting setting.

The runner collects every planned contrast, including nulls, under:
  results/reviewer/dmn_revision_checks/
and renders:
  results/reviewer/figures/dmn_simple_effects.png

No image/model failure or missing table is interpreted as zero clusters.

PHASE 2 — DMN RT SENSITIVITIES

Run only after the first phase's estimability checks pass. Dry run:

python3 code/run_revision_completion.py --phase rt --jobs 40

If that completes without an error, execute:

python3 code/run_revision_completion.py --phase rt --jobs 40 --execute

The two variants are deliberately separate from the corrected primary analysis:

all-trial-rt
  Keep the retained task EVs unchanged. Build both RT impulse EVs from every
  responded substantive decision row, not the incomplete event_RT companion
  rows. Their amplitudes are 1 and the recorded raw response_time.

response-duration
  Use the same all-trial RT EVs. In addition, change the six partner task/offer
  EV durations to the recorded offer-onset-to-response intervals. Keep their
  original onsets and amplitudes. Match every event to its retained EV by onset
  and require original duration/count agreement. All remaining regressors and
  interaction definitions remain unchanged. This is not a separate postresponse
  model and does not prove a pure deliberation mechanism.

Each variant includes all 47 participants and two runs: together, 188 L1 fits,
94 two-run L2 fits, and two six-contrast L3 fits. First-level concurrency is 40;
L2/L3 concurrency is capped at two. Thread limits prevent BLAS/OpenMP from
expanding each job into another large thread pool.

All 188 L1 designs are compiled and checked before any L1 fit starts. A failed
onset match, missing original input, or nonestimable focal contrast stops the
batch. A preparation failure may leave an incomplete scratch directory. Preserve
it for diagnosis and use a new --work-root after fixing the cause; do not delete
historical derivatives or force an overwrite.

Compiled checks, all group contrasts, compact maps, and provenance return to:
  results/reviewer/dmn_rt_sensitivity/

TIMING PROVENANCE

The task script defines rt = resp_onset - decision_onset, with decision_onset
recorded before the one-second offer-only display. Therefore BIDS raw RT already
includes that second. Do not add one second in the duration variant. The existing
behavioral trial builder subtracts response_delay for choice-window RT, consistent
with this distinction; the imaging variant uses the raw BIDS value directly.

Inspected source:
https://github.com/DVS-Lab/srndna-datapaper/blob/be25871ba5ac1983116183bf91aaff937f9f8f16/stimuli/psychopy/Ultimatum.py
SHA256: 18572b2985c188d50cba10096e29255651bf898651e40d0010cab37f678b1b74

That script has decision_dur=3, a one-second offer-only period, responses until
the three-second timer limit, and an additional 0.5-s display/feedback period.
The manuscript's shorthand "1 s + 2.5-s response window" therefore needs checking
against actual acquisition script versions; the entire remaining 2.5 s should
not automatically be called an active response window. The sensitivity code
uses recorded onsets/durations rather than hardcoding 3.5 s.

FIGURE 4 PARTICIPANT-SEM CANDIDATE

code/summarize_dmn_participant_bars.py uses corrected participant COPE/VARCOPE
extracts and the exact corrected group design. It fits nuisance slopes per
condition, uses inverse mean VARCOPE as a precision proxy, and computes weighted
participant dispersion divided by sqrt(effective N). This reduces to ordinary
SEM for equal weights, conditional on the fitted nuisance adjustment. It is not
the voxelwise FLAME estimator, and mean voxel VARCOPE is not the variance of an
ROI average without voxel covariance. These limits are recorded in provenance.

Reproduce the candidate with:
python3 code/summarize_dmn_participant_bars.py --plot

Outputs: results/reviewer/dmn_participant_bars/ and
results/reviewer/figures/dmn_weighted_participant_sem.png

The existing manuscript Figure 4 has not been silently replaced. Choose the
descriptive participant-SEM display or the correctly labeled FLAME display when
assembling the final figure; do not combine one estimator's means with another's
errors or treat the selected ROI as an independent inferential test.
