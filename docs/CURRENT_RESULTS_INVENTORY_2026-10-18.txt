SAMPLE AND BEHAVIOR
- Revised imaging/primary behavioral sample: 47 (25 younger, 22 older), not 50.
  Dataset-level enrollment and the paper's analyzed sample are distinct.
- Corrected primary social-choice analysis: 4,438 responded human-partner
  trials; participant random intercepts and offer slopes. The intended fit
  converges without singularity (see primary_optimizer_diagnostics.tsv).
  Offer coefficient 1.8013, SE .2373, p=3.17e-14 (reference age/partner).
  Offer x age p=.616; offer x age x similarity beta=.11358, SE=.09468, p=.230.
  This is no detected age moderation, not equivalence or proof that each
  individual was offer-sensitive. Do not substitute a human/computer contrast
  for the paper's similar/dissimilar contrast.
  Evidence: results/reviewer/tables/primary_acceptance_models.tsv;
  figures/acceptance_curves.png under results/reviewer.
- Ratings: 42 pre, 42 post, 41 paired, last-complete-attempt rule. No sub-143
  ratings; sub-144 ratings are not duplicates. Similar-minus-dissimilar ratings
  associations are exploratory. Pre-likeability vs differential offer slope:
  Spearman rho=-.405, p=.00772, BH q=.0366. Post-anger rho=.397, p=.00916,
  q=.0366. Corresponding Pearson associations do not survive BH (q=.0977).
  Trial-level moderation by pre fairness/likeability: p=.868/.834 in separate
  random-offer-slope fits; age moderation p=.203/.622. Do not present these as
  a demonstrated mechanism of the age effect.
  Evidence/figures: results/reviewer/ultimatum_ratings; results/reviewer/figures/
  ratings_choice_moderation.png and ratings_fairness_sensitivity_associations.png.
- Norm models remain exploratory. The positive-bias extension permits both
  accept- and reject-biased behavior. Its stable empirical fits still contain
  divergences, and six of eight conditional recovery fits need diagnostic
  review. workflow_status.json says parameter_recovery_validated=false and
  imaging_covariates_released=false. No norm-sensitivity/learning-rate covariate
  is presently cleared as an individual-difference imaging measure.
  Evidence: results/norm_learning/stan-bias-stable-v1/baseline_stable_comparison.tsv;
  results/norm_learning/stan-bias-validation-v1/workflow_status.json.

IMAGING: CORRECTED RT, THREE TIMING MODELS, STANDARDIZED DMN COVARIATES
- Historical Figure 4/ACC age claim is superseded. Files remain for provenance.
- DMN younger-positive similar-minus-dissimilar offer-slope connectivity:
  FLAME and randomise cluster t=2.6 detect effects in all three timing models;
  21 voxels overlap across both procedures and all three timings. TFCE p is
  .0149/.0558/.0392 (full-RT/pooled-post/partner-post); not all three pass TFCE.
  This is a within-younger effect, not by itself an age difference.
- DMN younger-minus-older: TFCE p=.0465/.0419/.0263. FLAME has no age cluster
  in full-RT or pooled-post; partner-post has 92 voxels, 35 overlapping TFCE.
  The age comparison is method/model sensitive. Unequal-variance audit pending.
  Evidence: results/dmn_covariate_standardized/v1/{imaging,randomise};
  new figures/influence: results/dmn_followup/v1/diagnostics after Linux run.
- Activation: age difference in association with the similar-minus-dissimilar
  acceptance intercept is the strongest current cross-method brain-behavior
  candidate. All three timing models pass randomise cluster t=2.6/3.1; at 3.1,
  61 voxels overlap across FLAME/randomise and all timings. TFCE p=.0852/.0460/
  .0373. Left parietal, approximately [-48,-48,48]. Acceptance intercept is NOT
  offer sensitivity, Gu norm sensitivity, or a general activation age effect.
- Activation versus differential offer sensitivity in older adults passes
  some permutation tests but does not spatially overlap FLAME detections.
  Keep as method-dependent, not convergent evidence.
- ECN: no significant permutation findings in the audited models. Do not
  reinstate the submitted ECN effect.
- Fixed-display supplemental task checks: positive task-wide offer slope
  TFCE p=.0324 (15 voxels), cluster t=3.1 p=.0364 (95 voxels). Human>computer
  task response TFCE p=.0006; computer>human p=.0045. These concern task/partner
  responses, not a replication of the social offer-slope age interaction.
  Human>computer offer-slope contrast does not survive permutation correction.
  Evidence: results/permutation_audit/v1/model_contrast_summary.tsv and maps;
  results/crossed_behavior_l3/v1/imaging; results/permutation_followup/v1/
  figures_and_extractions (activation overlays, scatterplots, influence).
