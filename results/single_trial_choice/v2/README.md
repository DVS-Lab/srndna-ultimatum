# Do brain signals explain acceptance differently by age?

This exploratory analysis uses the same 4,382 human choices from 47 participants in all model comparisons. Source and trial-order fingerprints come from the v1 manifest. A partial published run is excluded. The behavioral model includes offer amount by partner similarity by age, quadratic and cubic offer terms, block, run, position, sex, temporal SNR, motion and corrected response time. The RT terms distinguish participant means from trial deviations. Response time is measured at choice, so the analysis concerns observed decisions. All fits use a correlated participant intercept and offer slope.

The next block adds five single-trial LSS z measures, each divided into a run mean and a trial deviation. Three regions come from spatially corrected offer-slope maps: the medial frontal and precuneus components from the younger-greater-than-older contrast at Z > 2.6, and posterior visual cortex from the full-sample mean at Z > 3.1. The final two signals are the frontal target of the full-display DMN age contrast at Z > 2.6 and signed DMN expression estimated outside that target. The single-trial block tests the product of the two within-run signals. This is an interaction between simultaneous activation measurements.

Linux1 separately tests measured DMN gPPI from the opposite run in the corrected 49-voxel cluster. Its four terms comprise the coupling coefficient and interactions with offer and partner. The analysis compares that block with activation alone and with activation plus the same-trial interaction. Full-sample likelihood-ratio comparisons and participant-held-out log loss answer different questions. A positive change in held-out log loss reduction denotes an improvement.

Age moderation is tested by direct nested comparisons: five age-by-within-run-activation terms jointly, one age-by-DMN-by-target product after including age interactions with their main effects, and four age interactions with the opposite-run gPPI block. The latter model contains all lower-order terms. Equal-participant-weighted improvements are also summarized for each age group and as older minus younger, with conditional participant-bootstrap intervals that keep the fitted folds fixed. These multiple follow-ups are exploratory. Feature discovery used the entire imaging cohort, including the direct age maps. The held-out exercise validates regression coefficients conditional on the selected regions. An independent sample or repeat feature selection inside training folds is needed to validate the full discovery procedure.

Run `bash code/run_wholebrain_choice.sh` from the repository's main checkout on Linux1. Aggregate outputs and figure source tables are pushed to GitHub. Trial data remain in a private temporary directory.
