# Corrected social-versus-computer activation results

These two L3-only revision analyses reuse the 47 completed L2 outputs from the
task-wide offer-size pipeline and its corrected six-column FLAME 1+2 group
design. They were not part of the submitted inferential workflow.

## Cope 10: social task response versus computer

The historical contrast is `(similar + dissimilar) - 2 * computer`. The
positive direction contains five corrected clusters (756 voxels total), led
by bilateral inferior lateral-occipital/occipital-fusiform clusters. Smaller
clusters peak in subcallosal/medial orbitofrontal, right middle/inferior
frontal, and left lateral frontal cortex. The negative direction contains two
bilateral temporo-occipital fusiform/lingual clusters (282 voxels total).
Older > younger has no corrected clusters; younger > older has two clusters
in precuneus/posterior cingulate and posterior cingulate/precentral territory
(202 voxels total).

Because the human and computer conditions necessarily use different partner
images, the strong posterior visual and ventral-temporal effects are not a
specific assay of social cognition. This result is retained as a manipulation
and provenance check, not promoted to a manuscript figure.

## Cope 8: social offer-size slope versus computer

The historical contrast applies the same `(similar + dissimilar) - 2 *
computer` weighting to the three offer-size parametric effects. The positive
direction contains one 32-voxel cluster (cluster-corrected p = .023,
Zmax = 3.86; peak MNI [-7.31, -1.69, 11.7]). The cluster spans periventricular
left thalamic/caudate territory, white matter, and lateral-ventricle edge.
The negative direction and both age contrasts contain no corrected clusters.

The cluster is statistically valid under the recorded whole-brain threshold,
but its small, mixed-tissue, periventricular localization does not support a
clean mechanistic fairness-sensitivity claim. It remains an exploratory audit
result and is not promoted to the main manuscript.

All compiled designs, unthresholded and thresholded maps, cluster tables,
smoothness estimates, search masks, source paths, and hashes are retained in
this directory. Both analyses used Z > 3.1 and cluster-corrected p < .05.
