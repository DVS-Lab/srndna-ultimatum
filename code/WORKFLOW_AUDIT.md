# SRNDNA Ultimatum Game workflow and result audit

Last updated: 2026-09-07

This document records what can be established from the submitted manuscript,
tracked repository, and reviewer materials before any inferential result is
changed. It separates the submitted analysis from revision analyses and from
proposed server-side checks. The original `srndna-ultimatum` Git history remains
the production provenance record. Later revision artifacts were imported
selectively from a separate working repository without merging histories.

## Status vocabulary

- **Established**: directly supported by a tracked input, script, template, or
  submitted result and independently checked where feasible.
- **Provisional**: the best reconstruction from available evidence, but a
  production output or software record is still needed.
- **Unresolved**: the available sources disagree or do not identify the fact.
- **Revision analysis**: added in response to peer review; it is not presented
  as part of the originally submitted workflow.

The machine-readable companion is
`logs/records/manuscript-result-manifest.tsv`.

| Trial counts | Established/corrected | The 47-participant imaging sample has 94 runs and 6,768 trials: 48 trials per partner per participant. The recovered sub-144 run-01 events add one previously hidden miss, for 114 missed trials (1.68%). |
| Misses by age | Revision analysis, event-corrected | Younger participants missed 37 trials (mean 1.48/person); older participants missed 77 (mean 3.50/person). | Response-time origin | Established | The task delays response choices for 1 s. Cleaned behavioral RT subtracts this second and therefore measures response-selection latency. The first-level RT EV used the raw BIDS response time; its simultaneous constant and parametric RT EVs make the one-second shift an intercept change, not a change in the centered parametric regressor. |
| RT companion-event construction | Source and production identity match established | Among 6,654 responded trials, 930 (13.98%) lack a companion `event_RT` row. All 804 responded first trials of blocks lack that row; the other 126 omissions are every non-first trial in both runs for sub-143. The retained sub-143 L1 task blocks exactly match their own substantive rows, so sub-143 does not require repair. Both sub-144 runs match the historical duplicated events and use the companion-RT policy. The minimal repair corrects sub-144's task labels while retaining that policy; changing RT construction is a separate whole-sample sensitivity analysis. |
| Response-time result | Revision analysis, event-corrected | Human-partner response-selection RT is about 0.393 s overall. In a participant random-intercept/similarity-slope model, the older-group coefficient is -0.1187 s (SE 0.0556, p = 0.038); exact output is generated by the revision script. |
| Decision epoch | Established | The stimulus and selected response remain visible after a response. The modeled task duration therefore spans offer display/decision and post-response display; it cannot be described as pure deliberation time. |
| Intertrial interval | Established | Within-block gaps average about 0.765 s. Between-block gaps take approximately 8, 10, or 12 s. |
| Partner ratings | Revision analysis | Pre/post bargaining-rating logs are present for most but not all participants. Forty-one pre-task and 39 post-task records are complete and unique; four participant-session files contain appended duplicate administrations and are flagged rather than resolved post hoc. They can assess explicit ratings of the depicted partners but cannot validate the unmeasured belief that partners were truly similar/dissimilar. |

## Required read-only server audit

Local work must not fabricate or rerun FEAT outputs. On the Linux analysis
server, run the repository's read-only imaging audit against the production
derivative tree. It must capture:

1. the fMRIPrep dataset description, HTML boilerplate, container/image record,
   BOLD and group-output NIfTI headers, and FSL version;
2. the rendered first- and third-level `design.fsf`, `design.mat`, `design.con`,
   `design.grp`, cluster tables, and relevant log files;
3. input counts, participant order, cope mapping, covariate columns, rank,
   condition number, variance-inflation diagnostics, and group membership;
4. the exact submitted cluster-inference command, search mask, smoothness/GRF
   quantities, cluster extent and corrected p-values, and the minimum
   significant extent at the recorded Z = 3.1 and p = .05 parameters;
5. main effects, within-age simple effects, and the computer condition from
   already-estimated outputs before any new L3 contrast is proposed; and
6. actual first-level activation/nPPI design estimability and the complete RT
   event-construction chain, including the possible first-trial RT omission.

The audit is non-mutating. It does not prepare or run permutation/TFCE,
tSNR-removal, participant-deletion, or unified-sensitivity ECN analyses. After
the production audit, robust FLAME deweighting or genuinely missing L3
contrasts may be proposed in versioned directories, but only after a separate
scientific decision.

## Repository-size and cleanup boundary

The original `srndna-ultimatum` repository is already lean: before the revision
import its working tree was about 17 MiB and `.git` about 5 MiB. It does not
contain the unrelated RL/DDM fit archive that made the separate `srndna-ug`
history large. No history rewrite is needed or authorized here.

The full BIDS dataset remains outside Git. Only compact Ultimatum events and
Bargaining partner ratings needed by the revision workflow are retained under
`source_data/`. Existing compact text derivatives in `derivatives/` belong to
the original history and have not been bulk-deleted or reorganized during the
scientific audit.

## Reviewer-response interpretation guardrails

- Distinguish the submitted analysis from every revision analysis.
- Do not describe a nonsignificant interaction as evidence of equivalence or
  adequate power; report estimates, uncertainty, and limitations.
- Describe PPI as context-dependent functional connectivity, not effective or
  directional connectivity.
- Treat the computer condition as a nonsocial comparison and the cartoon
  partners as a social-context manipulation; do not claim successful belief or
  similarity induction without a direct measure.
- State that the focal clusters are small and inference is cluster-based; avoid
  overclaiming compensatory neural mechanisms from cross-sectional results.
- Preserve participant exclusions unless a documented data-integrity criterion
  requires otherwise. Influence checks are sensitivity analyses, not a license
  for post hoc exclusion.
